Privacy policy

Effective 29 December 2025 · Last updated 7 September 2026

ForFriendSake is a social app for friend groups that already know each other. This page explains what we collect to make squads, activities, check-ins, leaderboards, and chat work, why we collect it, and how it is protected. We do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

ForFriendSake (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is developed by Gurumanie Singh Dhiman. This policy applies to the ForFriendSake iOS app and this website. By using the app, you agree to this policy. If you do not, do not use the app.

Questions belong in Support or at support@forfriendsake.com.

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell it.

4. How it is visible to other people

Squad members see your username, profile photo, RSVPs, check-ins, and squad chat.

Leaderboards show squad names and points to other users of the app, worldwide and by country.

The map shows activity pins. If an activity belongs to a squad you are not in, you can request to join. Full chat and member detail stay with the squad.

This is not a product for meeting strangers, dating, or browsing open public squads.

6. Location

Location is read while you use the map or check in to an activity. Check-in only unlocks within 250 metres of the pin. Being further away is treated as “not there yet.” You can revoke location permission in iOS Settings. Without it, check-in and map features will not work fully.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest with our providers. Firestore security rules restrict reads and writes by account and squad membership. Invite codes expire, rotate, and cap uses. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

We do not claim end-to-end encryption of chat. Messages are stored so squadmates can read them.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information for as long as your account exists and as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal duties. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise personal information within 30 days, except where we must retain it for law.

You can start deletion from the app (Settings) or by emailing support@forfriendsake.com.

9. Your choices

  • Update profile details in the app.
  • Control location and notification permission on the device.
  • Leave a squad, which removes that squad’s chat and activity access.
  • Request a copy of your personal data, or deletion, by emailing support.

10. Children

ForFriendSake is age-gated 13+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Users aged 13 to 17 should use the app with parental awareness, in line with Apple’s age rating.

11. International transfers

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate, where privacy law may differ from your own. Using ForFriendSake means that transfer is part of the service.

12. California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the CCPA / CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct, and not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell personal information. To exercise a right, email support@forfriendsake.com.

13. Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date above. For significant changes we will try to notify you in the app on a later launch. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy. If you do not, stop using the app and ask us to delete your account.
