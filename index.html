Points for showing up.

ForFriendSake is an iPhone app for friend groups that keep saying “we should hang out.” Make a squad, pin a real place, and check in when you get there. Squads earn points for the meetups that happen, and rank against every other squad.

Download on the App Store Free · iPhone and iPad · iOS 26.1 or later

Only check-ins count.

Saying you’ll come is free. Points come from the people who turn up: check-in opens when your phone is within 250 metres of the pin, and a hangout only scores when enough of the squad checks in.

The score is worked out after the hangout ends, on ForFriendSake’s server rather than on anyone’s phone, so nobody can tap their way up the leaderboard.

How it works

  1. Start a squad

    Invite friends with an 8-character code. Codes change every 7 days and stop working after a set number of uses, so one that escapes the group chat goes dead.

  2. Plan at a real place

    Search Apple Maps, drop a pin, set a time. Your squad RSVPs and talks it over in a chat thread just for that plan.

  3. Turn up and check in

    Open the activity when you arrive and tap Check In. If you’re still on your way, the app waits for you.

  4. Climb the board

    Leaderboards rank squads worldwide and in your country, for this week and for all time.

The details

Sign in
Apple, Google or email. For ages 13 and up.
Chat
A thread for each squad and each plan, plus direct messages with anyone you share a squad with.
Location
Read while you’re using the map or checking in. You can turn it off in iOS Settings.
Your data
No ads, and nothing sold. The privacy policy lists everything that’s collected.
Appearance
Light or dark, with an accent colour you choose.
Requires
iOS 26.1 or later, on iPhone or iPad.
