The loop

Create or join a squad, plan something at a real place, show up, and let the server score the hangout.

1

Form a squad

Create one and invite friends with an 8-character share code. Codes rotate every 7 days and cap their redemptions, so a leak in a group chat stops working.

2

Plan something real

Pick a place through Apple Maps, pin it, and let squadmates RSVP. The hangout has a real address, not a vague maybe.

3

Show up and check in

GPS check-in unlocks within 250 metres of the pin. Being further away is treated as not there yet, not as an error.

4

Climb the board

After the hangout ends, the server scores it. Squads rank worldwide and in your country, weekly and all-time.

Built around showing up

The distinctive parts are turnout scoring and a map of what is actually happening. Everything else supports those two.

Points you cannot game

Scoring is settled entirely on the server after the hangout ends. Turnout drives the score, so a squad where everyone turns up scores dramatically more than one where half drift off.

  • An activity needs at least 2 check-ins to score at all.
  • Members returning after 3 or more weeks away earn a comeback bonus.

A map of what is happening

Activities appear as branded pins, filterable by type. Tap one for details. If it belongs to a squad you are not in, you can request to join.

Chat, three ways

A thread per squad, a thread per activity, and direct messages. DMs open automatically between people who already share a squad. Typing indicators, unread badges, and read state are included.

How points work

This is the honest version. Nobody on the client awards the score. After the window closes, the server looks at who RSVP’d and who checked in, then writes the result once.

Turnout is the lever

Full turnout scores in a different league from a half-empty room. Filling the room is the whole game.

Two people, minimum

A hangout with a single check-in does not score. It has to be a meetup, not a solo pin drop.

Coming back still counts

Drift off for three weeks or more, then show up again, and the squad still gets a comeback bonus. Leaving does not lock you out.

The rest of the kit

Sign in

Apple, Google, or email. Age-gated 13+.

Yours to theme

Light and dark in the app, plus 11 accent colours. This site follows suit — toggle light or dark from the icon in the header, always in the cream-and-coral mark.

Leaderboards

Squads ranked worldwide and within your country, weekly and all-time.

Requires

iOS 26.1 or later. iPhone and iPad.
